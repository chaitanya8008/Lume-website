About Lume · Elite Enterprises

Twenty years in glass. Seven years making our own mirrors.

Lume is the designer mirror brand of Elite Enterprises. We have been in the mirror and glass industry for over 20 years, and moved into manufacturing our own LED mirrors around seven years ago.

A workshop built for precision.

Our workshop in Panchkula is equipped with a state-of-the-art laser frosting machine, which lets us work with high precision while increasing our production capacity. Every mirror is cut, laser-frosted, wired and tested in-house, then packed and dispatched from the same unit.

Because we control every step, we can take on shapes and frosting patterns that off-the-shelf suppliers won't attempt, and keep quality consistent from a single mirror to a full project run. We use CE-certified components in our mirrors.

We supply builders and retailers across the Chandigarh Tricity, ship to homeowners pan-India, and frost glass for other mirror, glass and furniture businesses through our trade frosting service.
