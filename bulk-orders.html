One workshop, direct to your project.

Every mirror is cut, laser-frosted and wired in-house at our Panchkula workshop — no middlemen, no imported stock. That means consistent quality across a 100-unit run, a single point of accountability, and the freedom to match your project's exact design language: your shapes, your frosting artwork, even your brand's monogram etched into the glass.

Start with a single sample unit — approve the quality, finish and light colour in hand before committing to the full run. We have been in the mirror and glass industry for over 20 years and manufacturing LED mirrors for seven, and already supply builders and retailers across the Chandigarh Tricity.
