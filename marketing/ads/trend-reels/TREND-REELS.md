# Lume trend reels: hook-first, made for the algorithm

Five short (10 to 17 s) vertical reels in the formats that are doing well on Instagram and YouTube Shorts right now: a big caption hook in the first second, hard cuts on the beat, a "reveal", and an ending that asks people to comment or save. Comments, saves and rewatches are what push a reel to new people.

Every reel comes in **two versions**:
- `...mp4` has an original beat (copyright-free, safe for ads).
- `...-no-music.mp4` is silent. **Use this one for normal posts and add a trending sound inside Instagram.** Trending audio is the single biggest reach boost, and it changes every week, so pick it on the day you post (see below).

## How to add a trending sound (2 minutes)

1. Instagram → **+** → **Reel** → pick the `-no-music.mp4` file.
2. Tap the **music** icon → **Trending** (the ones with a ↗ arrow are rising right now). Good fits: upbeat instrumental or "satisfying" sounds for #1 and #4, a popular quiz/poll sound for #2 and #3, any calm trending track for #5.
3. Cut points in the videos land on a 120 BPM beat (every 0.5 s), so most pop and house tracks will line up.
4. **Business account note:** some songs are blocked for business accounts. If a song is greyed out, use one from the trending list that's available; they're all licensed.
5. Keep the caption short, put the hook in the first line, and **reply to every comment in the first hour**. That's when Instagram decides whether to push it further.

**Best times to post (India):** 12–2 pm or 7–10 pm. Post a new reel every 2–3 days instead of all five at once.
