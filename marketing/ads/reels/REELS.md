## Using them as Meta ads

In Ads Manager, for the campaigns in `../../ADS-PLAN.md`:
- **Click-to-WhatsApp (homeowners):** use Reels **02** and **03** for Reels & Stories placements (keep the square/portrait images from `../meta/` for Feed). Primary text: use the captions above without hashtags.
- **Business buyers:** use Reel **04**.
- **Awareness / retargeting:** Reel **01**.
- Leave room for Meta's overlays: all key text sits in the middle of the frame, so nothing important is hidden behind the caption, buttons or profile name.

Tip: post 01 and 02 as normal Reels first. Whichever gets more views in 3 to 4 days is the one to put ad money behind ("Boost" or Ads Manager).
