**Google new-advertiser credit:** Google regularly shows new Indian advertisers a "spend X, get X in credit" offer (₹20,000 has been common). It appears on the sign-up screen or under Billing → Promotions; spending must be completed within 60 days, and the credit then covers future clicks. The budget above spends about ₹18,000 a month on Google, so it would meet a ₹20,000 requirement in roughly five weeks. Check the exact offer on your screen before relying on it.

**How to judge it after 2–3 weeks:** look at cost per WhatsApp chat or quote request, not clicks. If a campaign brings chats at a cost you are happy with relative to your average order, raise its budget by about 20% a week. Pause keywords or ads that spend ₹1,500+ with no chats.
