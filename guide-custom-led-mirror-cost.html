Guide · Pricing

What actually decides the cost of a custom LED mirror?

We don't publish a price list — not as a sales tactic, but because made-to-order genuinely means every mirror is priced from its own drawing. Here is exactly what moves the number, so you can shape your design to your budget.

The six factors, in order of impact

1 · Size

The biggest lever. More glass area, a longer LED run, a larger frosting pass and heavier packing all scale with size. If you're balancing budget, decide the size last — small changes here move the price more than any feature toggle.

2 · Shape complexity

A rectangle or circle is a straightforward cut. Arches, clouds, waves and fully irregular outlines take longer to cut and finish safely, so they carry a premium over a simple shape of the same area — that's the cost of a mirror nobody else on your street has.

3 · Frosting pattern

The frosted pattern is drawn by our fibre laser at ±0.1 mm precision. A clean border frost is quick; a dense, intricate artwork keeps the laser running longer. Bringing your own pattern — a monogram, a motif from your tiles — is welcome and priced by its laser time, not by how unusual it is.

4 · Smart features

Each add-on has a hardware cost: the heated defogger pad, the combined clock + temperature + Bluetooth unit, and the 3× or 5× inset magnifier. The three-colour switchable lighting with touch dimming is standard on every Lume mirror — it doesn't cost extra.

5 · Frame

Frameless is the baseline. Aluminium frames — matte black, silver, champagne, white or sage — add material and finishing work, priced by the mirror's perimeter.

6 · Quantity

One mirror is priced as one mirror. A run of ten shares its setup time across ten units, and bulk project orders are priced at factory terms with GST invoicing — if you're a hotel, builder or retailer, say so in your enquiry, it changes the maths.

Delivery

Mirrors ship pan-India from our Panchkula workshop, packed for transit. Delivery cost depends on your city and the mirror's size — tell us your location with the enquiry and it's included in the quote.

How to get an accurate quote in one message

  1. The design — pick one from the collections or share your own reference on the custom page.
  2. Size in millimetres or inches.
  3. Light choice — 3-in-1 switchable (standard) or a fixed colour.
  4. Features — defogger, clock/Bluetooth unit, magnifier, frame colour.
  5. Your city, for delivery.
